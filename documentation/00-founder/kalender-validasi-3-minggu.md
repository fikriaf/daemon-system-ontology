# Kalender validasi 3 minggu (+ minggu 4 produksi v0.2)

Sesuai **Validation Kit v0.1** — workflow yang disarankan.

## Minggu 1

| Aktivitas | Owner | Output |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| Founder kirim **Paket A** ke Commercial Director & CGL leaders | Founder | Review 11 objek commercial dimulai |
| Founder kirim **Paket B** ke Finance Lead | Founder | Review 9 objek finance/governance dimulai |
| Parallel: stakeholder isi checklist per paket | Domain owners | Feedback tertulis |

## Minggu 2

| Aktivitas | Owner | Output |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| Founder kirim **Paket C** ke 6 Branch Heads | Founder | Review 16 objek ops/network (per cabang 2–3 jam) |
| Founder mulai **Paket D** (bukan marathon — 2–3 sesi refleksi) | Founder | Catatan Area 1–5 + 10 pertanyaan standalone |

## Minggu 3

| Aktivitas | Owner | Output |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| **Sesi konsolidasi** ~3 jam dengan stakeholder kunci | Founder | Keputusan ambigu diputus; isi [`log-keputusan-founder.md`](log-keputusan-founder.md) |
| Bahas perbedaan pandangan antar domain | Semua | Prioritas deep-validation untuk v0.2 |

Usulan agenda konsolidasi (dari kit): lihat [`agenda-sesi-founder.md`](agenda-sesi-founder.md).

## Minggu 4 — Catalog v0.2

| Aktivitas | Owner | Output |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| Produksi **Object Catalog v0.2** dengan tracked changes | Data / ontology team | Dokumen v0.2 |
| Distribusi untuk **spot-check** (bukan full review ulang) | Founder | Feedback 1 minggu |
| Tetapkan v0.2 sebagai baseline **ADR** dan **Wave 1** | Founder | Sign-off changelog v0.2; ownership matrix |

## Aturan efisiensi (Founder tetapkan di muka)

Validation Kit merekomendasikan Founder menetapkan rules sebelum konsolidasi, misalnya:

- Siapa memutus jika Commercial vs Operations bentrok?
- Apakah perubahan v0.2 memerlukan CEO sign-off atau cukup Business Owner?

Catat rules di bagian bawah [`log-keputusan-founder.md`](log-keputusan-founder.md) setelah sesi.
